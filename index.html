AI-Powered Patient Scheduling & Authorization Assistant

A smart system to improve hospital scheduling and insurance processing

Problem AI Solution Implementation Impact Ethics

Problem

Hospitals and clinics face long wait times due to manual scheduling and insurance verification processes. Staff must spend time checking coverage, submitting prior authorizations, and scheduling appointments, which slows down patient care.

AI Solution

An AI-powered assistant that automates patient scheduling, insurance verification, and prior authorization. It also includes a chatbot to answer patient questions quickly.

Implementation Plan

The system uses HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and AI APIs. It connects to hospital databases to check insurance, schedule appointments, and process authorization requests automatically.

Impact

This system reduces wait times, improves accuracy, and helps hospital staff work more efficiently. Patients receive faster care and better communication.

Ethics & Challenges

Important concerns include patient privacy, data security (HIPAA compliance), and potential AI bias. Challenges include system integration and staff training.
